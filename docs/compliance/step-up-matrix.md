## Reasons enforced inside services (`DualControlGate`)

These are not visible as annotations on the handler. The endpoint itself may carry a single-factor `@RequiresStepUp` (for example `OPERATOR_USER_ADMIN`); the service then demands the second approver when the condition holds.

| Reason | Endpoint | Second approver | When |
|---|---|---|---|
| `OPERATOR_USER_INVITE` | `POST /api/v1/admin/users` | yes (not while fewer than two enrolled administrators exist: bootstrap) | granting a gated operator role (REGISTRY_ADMIN, COMPLIANCE_OFFICER, SUPPORT_AGENT) |
| `OPERATOR_USER_ROLES` | `PATCH /api/v1/admin/users/{userId}/roles` | yes (not while fewer than two enrolled administrators exist: bootstrap) | granting or removing a gated role |
| `OPERATOR_USER_ENABLE` | `POST /api/v1/admin/users/{userId}/enable` | yes (not while fewer than two enrolled administrators exist: bootstrap) | privileged account |
| `OPERATOR_USER_DISABLE` | `POST /api/v1/admin/users/{userId}/disable` | yes (not while fewer than two enrolled administrators exist: bootstrap) | privileged account |
| `OPERATOR_USER_DELETE` | `DELETE /api/v1/admin/users/{userId}` | yes (not while fewer than two enrolled administrators exist: bootstrap) | privileged account |
| `OPERATOR_USER_REINSTATE` | `POST /api/v1/admin/users/{userId}/enable` | yes | account that an access review revoked |
| `DORA_INCIDENT_DOWNGRADE` | `POST /api/v1/dora/incidents/{id}/classify` | yes | downgrading an active MAJOR incident |
| `DORA_INCIDENT_CLOSE` | `PATCH /api/v1/dora/incidents/{id}/status` | yes | closing an active MAJOR incident |
| `CLIENT_CLASSIFICATION_DOWNGRADE` | `POST /api/v1/entities/{id}/classification` | yes | moving a client to a less protective MiFID II category |
