# Investor Overview

As an investor on the eWpG Registry, you have read and reporting access to all the security tokens registered in your name. This section describes what investors can see and do on the platform.

## What investors can do

- **View holdings** — see all security tokens held in your registered wallets
- **Track transfers** — view your complete transfer history with links to block explorers
- **Connect wallets** — register one or more blockchain wallets with your account
- **Download statements** — export your holdings or transfer history as PDF or CSV
- **View token details** — see the issuer, ISIN, token standard, and contract address for each holding

## What investors cannot do

- Initiate token transfers (transfers happen on-chain, directly between wallets, or via the issuer)
- Submit or review KYC documents (handled by the registry operator on your behalf)
- Modify any registry records

:::note
The eWpG Registry is a **read-only** view of your on-chain token holdings. To transfer security tokens, you interact directly with the blockchain using your wallet, or via a broker/transfer agent as specified in the issuance terms.
:::

## ONCHAINID and KYC

To receive ERC-3643 security tokens, you must have a valid **ONCHAINID** and current KYC/AML claims. The registry operator creates your ONCHAINID automatically when you are onboarded and issues claims after your KYC review is complete.

You can view your ONCHAINID status under **Profile → Identity**. The page shows:
- Your ONCHAINID contract address
- The network it is deployed on
- Which claims are currently valid and their expiry dates

## Getting started

1. Complete [onboarding](../onboarding) and KYC review
2. [Set up your wallet](./wallet-setup) and connect it to your account
3. Ask your issuer to whitelist your wallet for their token
4. [View your investments](./viewing-investments) once tokens are transferred to your wallet
